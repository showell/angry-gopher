Learn to play Lyn Rummy

Lyn Rummy is a two-player card game played with two decks. You can play it at a kitchen table with real cards, or right here at /game — it's the same game either way. This page teaches the rules from scratch.

The three legal melds

Players build stacks of cards on a shared board. A stack is legal when it has three or more cards and forms one of three shapes: 

at (10,10): A♠ A♥ A♦
Set — same value, all different suits. Never longer than four cards (one per suit).
at (10,10): 5♣ 6♣ 7♣
Pure run — three or more consecutive values in a single suit.
at (10,10): 9♥ 10♠ J♦
Red/black run — consecutive values whose colors alternate red and black.

Try it

Each loose card below belongs in exactly one of the three stacks. Drag every card home to finish all three melds. Grab a card to drag it; drop it on a stack to join that stack.
