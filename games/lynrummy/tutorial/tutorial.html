Learn to play Lyn Rummy

Lyn Rummy is a two-player card game played with two decks. You can play it at a kitchen table with real cards, or right here at /game — it's the same game either way. This page teaches the rules from scratch.

The three legal melds

Players build stacks of cards on a shared board. A stack is legal when it has three or more cards and forms one of three shapes. On a kitchen table you'd lay each meld out in front of you — the examples below each sit on their own little table.

Set — same value, all different suits

Three or four cards of the same value. No suit may repeat, so a set never grows past four cards — one per suit. 

at (0,0): A♠ A♥ A♦
at (0,0): 7♣ 7♦ 7♥ 7♠

Pure run — consecutive values, one suit

Three or more consecutive values, all in a single suit. Runs can grow as long as the deck allows. 

at (0,0): 5♣ 6♣ 7♣
at (0,0): 9♥ 10♥ J♥ Q♥ K♥

Red/black run — consecutive values, alternating colors

Three or more consecutive values whose colors alternate red and black (the suits themselves don't matter, only the colors). 

at (0,0): 9♥ 10♠ J♦
at (0,0): 3♠ 4♥ 5♣ 6♦ 7♣

Try it

Each loose card below belongs in exactly one of the three stacks. Drag every card home to finish all three melds. Grab a card to drag it; drop it on a stack to join that stack.
